## Customer-facing behavior

- Newly added models have no numeric repair price, no promised repair time and no preassigned warranty. They link directly to a repair enquiry and generate no extra model-specific pages.
- The calculator offers explicit enquiry types for these models. It cannot inherit older generic specialist prices or expose unverified upgrades through a category fallback.
- Samsung phone models are grouped into S, A and Z families. Pixel generation groups derive from the catalogue so future additions cannot disappear from the page.
- “MacBook Battery Replacement” is retained as a pricing record but excluded from model selectors. The existing iPad 9th Gen digitizer price can now be selected under screen repair.
- Device and brand links open the corresponding quote model list. URL selections are validated, repeated values are ignored, and a requested repair is applied only after the customer chooses a compatible model. Starting over clears the selection.

## Launch and maintenance limits

The catalogue is a selection of repair enquiries, not an exhaustive list of every historical device or regional variant. Customers with an unlisted model should contact the team. Manufacturer announcements establish a device's identity and release status, never this business's ability to repair it.

The business owner must confirm existing price estimates, part descriptions, assessment charges, turnaround times and warranties against current supplier availability before public launch. Confirm feasibility for each enquiry before accepting a device. Recheck new releases and supplier options when adding models; preserve quote-only status until those details are verified.
